        SmartGP

        A Clearer Way Through Grant of Probate

        SmartGP helps executors navigate the Grant of Probate process with greater clarity and structure during a difficult time.

        A Grant of Probate gives the executor named in a valid Will the legal authority to manage and distribute the deceased’s estate according to the Will. SmartGP supports the journey from document preparation through to the probate application process.

        Start Your Application NowTalk to Us

        Why Grant of Probate Matters

        The Legal Authority to Move Forward

        A Grant of Probate allows the appointed executor to legally access, manage and administer the deceased’s estate. It is an important step before many estate ../Assets can be released, transferred or distributed.

        Access Estate Assets

        Gain the legal authority needed to access and manage estate assets.

        Settle Estate Matters

        Handle outstanding debts, liabilities and other estate obligations.

        Manage & Transfer Assets

        Carry out the necessary administration and transfer of estate assets.

        Distribute to Beneficiaries

        Distribute the remaining estate according to the instructions in the Will.

        Guidance Through Every Stage

        SmartGP simplifies the probate journey by helping executors understand what is required, organise the necessary documents and move through the application process in a more structured manner.

        01 GATHER

        Collect the Required Documents

        Prepare the original Will, death certificate, identification details and relevant estate information.

        02 REVIEW

        Review & Prepare the Application

        Submitted information and documents are reviewed and organised for the application.

        03 APPLY

        Submit the Probate Application

        Required probate documents are prepared and submitted through the legal process.

        04 ADMINISTER

        Manage & Distribute the Estate

        Once the Grant is issued, the executor can manage, transfer and distribute the estate.

        Prepare the Essential Documents

        Having the relevant documents ready helps make the probate application smoother and more organised.

        Original Will

        The deceased’s original signed and valid Will document for the probate application.

        Death Certificate

        The deceased’s official death certificate or certified copy confirming the date of death.

        Identity Verification

        Identification details of the deceased, executor(s) and beneficiaries for probate.

        Assets & Liabilities

        A complete record of estate assets, liabilities and supporting information.

        SmartLA

        When Estate Administration Needs a Different Path

        SmartLA helps families navigate the Letters of Administration process when a loved one passes away without a valid Will or when no executor is available to act.

        Letters of Administration gives an appointed administrator the legal authority to manage the deceased’s estate and proceed with the necessary administration and distribution.

        Start Your Application NowTalk to Us

        When SmartLA May Be Required

        Helping Families Move Forward Without an Executor

        SmartLA may be required when the estate cannot proceed through a standard Grant of Probate application. It provides a structured pathway for an eligible person to obtain the legal authority needed to administer the estate.

        No Valid Will

        The deceased passed away without leaving a valid Will that can be used for estate administration.

        No Appointed Executor

        There is no executor named or available to take responsibility for administering the estate.

        Executor Unable to Act

        The appointed executor is unable, unwilling or no longer available to carry out the required responsibilities.

        Estate Administration Is Required

        A legally authorised administrator is needed before the estate assets can be properly managed, transferred and distributed.

        From Estate Information to Legal Administration

        SmartLA provides a structured process to help families organise the required documents, understand the application requirements and move through the Letters of Administration process with greater clarity and confidence.

        01

        GATHER

        Prepare the Estate Information

        Collect death certificate, ID documents, family details and information relating to the deceased’s estate.

        02

        ASSESS

        Review the Estate & Family Details

        Documents, family relationships and estate information are reviewed and organised for application preparation.

        03

        PROCEED

        Prepare the LA Application

        Required documents are prepared and the application moves through the legal and administrative process.

        04

        ADMINISTER

        Manage & Distribute the Estate

        Once Letters of Administration is granted, the administrator can manage, transfer and distribute the estate.

        Documents to Get Started

        Having the necessary documents, family details, and estate information ready helps make the application process smoother and more organised from the start.

        Death Certificate

        The deceased’s official death certificate confirming the passing.

        Identification Details

        ID details of the deceased and involved family members.

        Family Records

        Documents showing the deceased’s family relationships and beneficiaries.

        Estate Information

        Full record of estate assets, liabilities, and supporting info.
