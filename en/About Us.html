SmartWills Malaysia

SmartWills Malaysia is a digital Will-writing platform that helps individuals prepare their Will in a simple, secure, affordable and accessible way—anytime, anywhere.

Who Are We?

We are a Wealth FinTech company dedicated to meeting today’s demand for financial services that are easy, fast and trusted. SmartWills was created through the combined expertise of financial advisors, financial planners, legal professionals and technology specialists.

SmartWills Berhad is the parent company of SmartWills Malaysia, providing the strategic foundation for the Group’s growth, technology innovation and regional expansion.

SmartWills Malaysia is our digital estate-planning brand, developed to make Will preparation simpler, more affordable and accessible to individuals from different financial backgrounds.

In late 2016, we began developing a user-friendly Will-making platform designed for both web and mobile users. After nearly three years of planning, market research and software development, we launched our online Will-writing service in Malaysia in early 2019.

As our customer base continued to grow, we expanded our regional presence by establishing a branch office in Singapore in mid-2023.

What Guide Us?
