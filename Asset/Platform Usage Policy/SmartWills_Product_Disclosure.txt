3. What is a Will?
3. 什么是遗嘱

A Will is a written expression of your true intention to dispose of your personal lawful property upon your death, made in the statutory form required by the Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China. A valid will allows you to arrange the distribution of your personal assets instead of default statutory succession.

遗嘱是自然人按照《中华人民共和国民法典》规定的法定形式，作出的在其死亡后处分个人合法财产的真实意思表示。有效的遗嘱可以优先于法定继承安排您的个人财产分配。


4. Who is eligible to make a Will?
4. 谁可以订立遗嘱

Any natural person who meets all the following requirements may make a will:

符合以下全部条件的自然人可以订立遗嘱：

• Has full capacity for civil conduct and sound mind;
• 具备完全民事行为能力、神志清楚；

• Aged 18 years old or above;
• 年满十八周岁；

• Able to understand the nature and legal consequence of making a will.
• 能够理解订立遗嘱的性质与法律后果。

9. What are the inherent legal risks in making a will?
9. 订立遗嘱本身固有的法律风险

9.1 A will cannot override designated beneficiary arrangements under insurance contracts, provident fund, annuity or other financial products. Such assets shall be paid to the designated beneficiary according to the relevant contract terms.
9.1 遗嘱不能变更保险、公积金、年金或其他金融产品已指定受益人的安排，该类资产按照对应合同约定给付指定受益人。

9.2 If a beneficiary is a minor at the time of your death, the minor’s share shall be managed by his/her guardian in accordance with law until the minor reaches the age of 18.
9.2 若遗嘱受益人在您身故时为未成年人，该未成年人对应的遗产份额依法由其监护人代为管理，直至其年满十八周岁。

9.3 Statutory reserved portions for persons without labour capacity and without source of income must be reserved, otherwise the relevant part of the will may be invalid.
9.3 遗嘱应当为缺乏劳动能力又没有生活来源的继承人保留必要的遗产份额，否则对应部分可能无效。

11. Who can be my Will Executor?
11. 谁可以担任遗嘱执行人

A will executor must be a natural person with full capacity for civil conduct, who agrees to accept the appointment and is capable of administering your estate. Legal persons or professional institutions may also act as the executor.

遗嘱执行人应当是具备完全民事行为能力、同意接受委任、具备遗产管理能力的自然人；法人、专业机构也可担任遗嘱执行人。


12. How many Executor(s) do I need?
12. 需要几名遗嘱执行人

You may appoint one or multiple executors. It is recommended to appoint at least one alternate executor.

您可以指定一名或多名遗嘱执行人，建议同步指定候补执行人。


13. Who can be my witness?
13. 谁可以担任见证人（适用于打印遗嘱 / 代书遗嘱）

Witnesses must be natural persons with full capacity for civil conduct. Beneficiaries, heirs, creditors, debtors and other interested parties may NOT act as witnesses. At least two witnesses are required for print wills and attested wills under the Civil Code.

见证人必须是具备完全民事行为能力的自然人；受益人、继承人、债权人、债务人及其他利害关系人不得担任见证人。依据民法典，打印遗嘱、代书遗嘱需要至少两名见证人。


14. How does the Will provide for distribution of assets?
14. 资产分配方式

模板支持按百分比分配，也支持指定单项财产分配。


15. What is meant by “Last Will & Testament”?
15. “最后一份遗嘱”含义

A valid will automatically revokes any prior will made by you. The latest valid will shall prevail unless it is lawfully revoked.

一份合法有效的遗嘱自动撤销您之前订立的遗嘱；以时间在后、符合法定要件的有效遗嘱为准，除非该遗嘱被依法撤销。
